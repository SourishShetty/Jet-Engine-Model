# Design and Development of a Jet Engine Model

**Bachelor's Thesis | Mechanical Engineering | 2024**

[**Read the Complete Project Report (PDF)**](docs/Jet_Engine_Bachelors_Thesis.pdf)

## Project Overview

This project involved the design, fabrication, assembly, and testing of a small-scale jet engine model as part of our Bachelor's degree in Mechanical Engineering at K. J. Somaiya College of Engineering, Somaiya Vidyavihar University.

The objective was to apply principles of fluid mechanics, thermodynamics, mechanical design, and manufacturing to the development of a working propulsion demonstrator.

The work covered component design, CAD modelling, airflow simulation, material selection, manufacturing processes, final assembly, and experimental observations.

## Engineering Work

### Mechanical Design and Component Development

The model incorporated an impeller, stator, motor assembly, combustion chamber, outer casing, fuel delivery arrangement, and exhaust nozzle.

CAD models were developed to examine component geometry, packaging, and assembly interfaces before fabrication.

### Airflow Simulation

A flow simulation was performed to investigate airflow behaviour through the model, including velocity trajectories and the intended swirling motion.

The simulation supported the design assessment, although the report does not establish a comprehensive experimental validation of the flow predictions.

### Fabrication and Assembly

The project combined 3D-printed components with fabricated metal parts.

Manufacturing activities included drilling, polishing, deburring, welding, and mechanical assembly. Particular attention was given to the combustion chamber, its integration with the outer casing, and the final assembly of the propulsion model.

### Testing and Observations

The completed model was assembled and tested, with observations documented in the thesis report.

The report includes photographs of the working model, airflow simulation results, measured model weight, and thrust-related testing.
